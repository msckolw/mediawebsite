## ⚖️ Fair Use Considerations

### When You Might Have Fair Use Defense:

Your use may qualify as fair use (17 U.S.C. § 107) if:

1. **Purpose:** News reporting and commentary (✅ You qualify)
2. **Nature:** Factual rather than creative work
3. **Amount:** Small portion of original work
4. **Effect:** Doesn't harm market for original

**However:** Fair use is complex and fact-specific. When in doubt:
- **Removal is safer than dispute**
- Consult with legal counsel for significant claims
- Consider licensing for ongoing use

### Fair Use is NOT a Defense When:
- Using entire high-resolution images without permission
- Using images that are the "heart" of the work
- Using creative/artistic works (photos, illustrations)
- Competing with rights holder's market
- Using content purely for decoration (not news reporting)

## 🔍 Preventing Future Claims

### Content Sourcing Best Practices:

#### **For Images:**
1. **Source from original news articles** (most defensible)
2. **Use Creative Commons licensed images** (check license terms)
3. **Purchase stock photos** (get license for commercial use)
4. **Use images from press releases** (usually permitted)
5. **Create original graphics** where possible

#### **Attribution Requirements:**
- Always credit photographer/source when known
- Link back to original article/source
- Use caption: "Image: [Source] / [Photographer]"

#### **What to Avoid:**
- ❌ Google Image Search results
- ❌ Social media images without permission
- ❌ Professional photography without license
- ❌ Images from stock photo sites without purchase
- ❌ Celebrity/paparazzi photos (high risk)

## 🎯 Special Situations

### If Claim Involves:

#### **1. Professional Photography**
- **Risk:** HIGH
- **Action:** Remove immediately unless licensed
- **Consider:** Offering to license for future use

#### **2. Agency Photos (Getty, AP, Reuters)**
- **Risk:** VERY HIGH (they actively enforce)
- **Action:** Remove immediately
- **Note:** These agencies are litigious

#### **3. User-Generated Social Media Content**
- **Risk:** MEDIUM
- **Action:** Verify permission or remove
- **Note:** Just because it's on Twitter doesn't mean you can use it

#### **4. News Wire Photos**
- **Risk:** HIGH if no subscription
- **Action:** Remove unless you have wire service subscription

#### **5. Historical/Archive Photos**
- **Risk:** VARIABLE (check if in public domain)
- **Action:** Verify copyright status before use

## 💼 When to Consult Legal Counsel

### Immediately Consult Lawyer If:

1. **Formal lawsuit filed** (not just cease & desist letter)
2. **Claim involves large damages** (over ₹1,00,000 / $1,200)
3. **Multiple claims from same entity**
4. **Agency enforcement action** (Getty Images, AFP, Reuters)
5. **Government content claims**
6. **Dispute over fair use with substantial amounts at stake**
7. **Counter-notification received after takedown**

### Lawyer NOT Immediately Needed For:

- Single informal claim from individual
- First-time infringement claim
- Willing to remove content immediately
- Clear-cut case where you're at fault
